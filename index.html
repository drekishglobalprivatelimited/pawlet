Feeding & Care

Treat them right

Store it right

Keep in a cool, dry place away from moisture and heat. Close the zip after every use.

Enjoy it fresh

Consume within 15 days of opening. Best before 12 months from packaging date.

Keep water close

Always give your pet access to clean drinking water after dehydrated treats.

Treat, not meal

Supplementary food. Follow the daily serving guide for your pet's size.
